# Swan

![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Mobile-friendly-purple)

A single-file anti-stress clicker where you tap a swan until the egg is completely knocked out of it. Works online and offline in the browser.

## Play

### Online

[Click here to play the Swan game online.](https://limafresh.github.io/swan/swan.html)

### Offline

Download the `swan.html` file to play in your browser without internet connection on any device.

![Screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/limafresh/swan/main/screenshot.png)

## Features

+ Click on the palette to change the color of the swan.
